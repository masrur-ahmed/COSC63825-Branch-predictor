# Load/store queue behaviour.
#  1. Store-to-load forwarding: the store's address is known early but its
#     data comes from a 6-cycle multiply; the load waits and then takes the
#     data from the store in 1 cycle instead of reading memory.
#  2. Memory disambiguation: the second store's address comes from a chain of
#     adds, so the younger load (address known at once) speculatively reads
#     memory first.  When the store's address resolves to the same location
#     the load and everything after it are squashed and re-executed.
               # of rs    Cycles in EX    Cycles in Mem    # of FUs
Integer adder    2          1                                1
FP adder         2          2                                1
FP multiplier    2          6                                1
Load/store unit  6          1               3                1

ROB entries = 32
R1=8, F1=2.0, F2=5.0
Mem[8]=1.5, Mem[16]=7.0

Addi R2, R2, 4
Addi R2, R2, 4
